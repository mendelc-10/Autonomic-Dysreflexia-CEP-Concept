Autonomic Dysreflexia Volunteer Module

Autonomic Dysreflexia (AD) is a medical complication that can be present in individuals with upper spinal cord injuries. As PACE volunteers who will be interacting with people in that demographic it is important to be aware of AD and what steps to take if a participant is suspected or confirmed to be having an episode. In this module you will learn the neurobiological mechanisms of AD, the possible symptoms, and steps to take if an episode is suspected.

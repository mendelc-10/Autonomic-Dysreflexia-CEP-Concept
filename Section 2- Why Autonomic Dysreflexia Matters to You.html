What Could Trigger AD during a MacWheelers session?

Exercise and transfers can introduce sources of pressure, irritation, or injury that may trigger AD.

Equipment and Straps

Check that straps and equipment are positioned appropriately and are not creating unnecessary pressure.

Skin Irritation or Injury

Be aware of possible friction, pressure points, or injuries during exercise.

Positioning and Transfers

Be attentive to positioning and potential pressure or injury during transfers between exercise stations.

As a Volunteer...

  • Stay alert to potential triggers during exercise.
  • Check in with your participant and take their concerns seriously.
  • Remember that AD may not always have obvious or visible symptoms.
